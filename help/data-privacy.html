How is my order and inventory data used?

Stockloop only uses your order history to calculate sales velocity and reorder suggestions for your own store — it is never shared, sold, or pooled across merchants. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export or delete your data anytime from Settings → Data.

Does uninstalling delete my data?

Uninstalling stops the app from running but retains your data for 30 days (in case you reinstall), then permanently deletes it. Request immediate deletion anytime via Settings → Data → Delete my data, or by emailing support.
