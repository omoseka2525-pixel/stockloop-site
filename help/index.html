Getting Started

How do I install Stockloop and get my first reorder suggestions?

Click "Add app" on the Shopify App Store listing, approve the permissions screen, and you're done — there's no setup wizard to complete. Stockloop automatically imports your last 12 months of order history the moment you install, and it starts calculating sales velocity immediately. Most stores see their first reorder suggestions within a few minutes, though very large catalogs (10,000+ SKUs) can take up to an hour for the initial import to finish. You'll get an email the moment your first suggestions are ready. You don't need to connect suppliers or import anything manually before this happens — that comes next, and only for SKUs where you want Stockloop to generate a purchase order automatically.

Do I need to do anything before Stockloop can work?

No. Stockloop reads your existing order and product history the moment you install, so reorder suggestions appear without configuration. Two optional steps improve accuracy fast: add supplier lead times (Suppliers tab) so suggestions account for how long stock takes to arrive, and set a safety-stock buffer per product or collection if you want more or less cushion than the default. Neither is required to start.

What Shopify plan or POS setup do I need?

Stockloop works on any Shopify plan and does not require Shopify POS or POS Pro. If you sell in multiple physical or online locations, Stockloop supports multi-location inventory and reorder suggestions natively — no separate setup per location.

Which permissions does Stockloop request, and why?

Stockloop requests read access to products, inventory levels, and order history (to calculate velocity and suggestions), and write access to draft purchase orders it creates on your behalf. It never modifies your product listings, prices, or storefront content.

Can I use Stockloop alongside my current inventory or accounting app?

Yes. Stockloop only manages purchase orders, suppliers, and reorder suggestions — it doesn't touch pricing, fulfillment, or accounting. In v1, it does not sync to accounting software; export PO and receiving data as CSV if your bookkeeper needs it.

Migrating from Stocky

Stocky is shutting down August 31, 2026 — will Stockloop import my Stocky data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Import from Stocky and upload the CSV exports for suppliers, products, and purchase order history (export these from Stocky before it shuts down — Stocky does not let Shopify migrate this for you automatically). Stockloop reconciles the import against your live Shopify catalog and flags any SKU or supplier it can't match so you can fix it manually. Do this well before August 31 — once Stocky is gone, its data cannot be recovered from anywhere but your own exports.

Which Stocky exports do I need, and how do I get them?

From Stocky, export: Suppliers (CSV), Purchase Orders (CSV), and Reorder/Min-Max settings if you use them. Stocky's own export tools are the only source for this — Shopify does not retain it after shutdown. We recommend exporting everything by September 30, 2026 even if you're not ready to switch yet, simply so the data exists somewhere safe.

Will my Stocky reorder points and min/max settings carry over exactly?

Stockloop reads imported min/max values as a starting point, but it recalculates suggestions going forward using its own velocity-and-lead-time model rather than freezing your old static thresholds forever. This usually improves accuracy within a few weeks, but expect the first suggestions after migration to shift somewhat from what Stocky showed.

I can't export something from Stocky (e.g. old POs) — is it lost?

If a field genuinely isn't exportable from Stocky, it will not be recoverable after August 31, 2026 — this is a Stocky limitation, not a Stockloop one. Export what you can now (suppliers and PO history are the most commonly missed). If you're stuck on a specific export, message us and we'll walk you through Stocky's export screens directly.

Does Stockloop cost more than Stocky, which was free?

Stockloop is a paid app because it's independently built and maintained (Stocky was subsidized as part of Shopify POS Pro). Pricing is flat — Free, $29, $59, or $99/month depending on catalog size and locations — with no per-order or per-PO fees, so your cost doesn't scale with sales volume the way some alternatives' usage-based pricing does.

Reorder Suggestions

How does Stockloop calculate a reorder suggestion?

Three inputs: (1) your sales velocity — units sold per day, averaged over a rolling window (default 30 days, configurable to 14, 60, or 90); (2) the supplier's lead time in days, from the Suppliers tab; (3) a safety-stock buffer, a number of extra days of cover you set per product or collection (default 7 days). The suggested reorder quantity covers velocity × (lead time + safety stock) minus what you already have on hand and on order. You can see the exact numbers behind any suggestion by clicking it.

Why did my velocity window jump around after a sale or stockout?

A short window (14 days) reacts fast to real demand shifts but can be skewed by a promo spike or a stockout that suppressed sales. Switch to a 60 or 90-day window for steadier products, or manually exclude a date range (Settings → Velocity → Exclude dates) if a specific promotion or outage shouldn't count.

A suggestion looks wrong — how do I check why?

Click the suggestion to open its breakdown: velocity used, window, lead time, safety stock, and current stock/on-order quantities. Most "wrong" suggestions trace to a missing or outdated supplier lead time, or a velocity window that includes an unusual spike or stockout. Fix the underlying input and the suggestion recalculates automatically — you don't need to edit the suggestion itself.

Can I override a suggested quantity?

Yes, always. Open the suggestion or the draft PO and edit the quantity directly before sending — overrides never get overwritten by recalculation once a PO is created. If you frequently override the same product the same way, consider adjusting its safety-stock buffer instead so future suggestions start closer to what you actually want.

What's a sensible safety-stock setting?

As a starting point: fast-moving, reliable-supplier products can use 3–7 days; slow-moving or unreliable-lead-time products (especially imported goods) often need 14–30 days. There's no universal number — it should reflect how much lead-time variability and demand volatility you actually see for that product.

Does Stockloop account for seasonality?

Not automatically in v1 — the velocity calculation is a rolling average, not a seasonal forecast. For seasonal products, shorten the velocity window as the season approaches, or manually adjust safety stock upward ahead of known peaks (e.g., before a holiday).

Why does a new product have no reorder suggestion?

New products need sales history before Stockloop can calculate a velocity. Until then, set a manual reorder point (Product → Reorder settings) so Stockloop can still suggest a quantity based on your estimate rather than waiting for data.

Can I set different lead times per supplier for the same product?

Yes — lead time is set at the supplier level and applied to whichever supplier is assigned as primary for that product. If a product has backup suppliers with different lead times, switching the primary supplier updates the suggestion accordingly.

Purchase Orders

How do I create and send a purchase order?

From a reorder suggestion, click "Create PO" — it pre-fills supplier, products, and quantities. Review and edit as needed, then click "Send" to email it directly to the supplier's contact on file as a PDF, or download it to send yourself. You can also start a PO from scratch (Purchase Orders → New) without a suggestion.

Can I edit a PO after it's been sent?

Yes — editing a sent PO doesn't automatically re-notify the supplier, so re-send it (or tell them by email/phone) after making changes so both records match.

Can I split one PO across multiple suppliers?

No — a PO is tied to one supplier, since it's what gets emailed to them. If products come from different suppliers, Stockloop generates a separate suggested PO per supplier automatically.

Can I duplicate a past PO?

Yes — open any past PO and click "Duplicate" to create a new draft with the same supplier and line items, ready to adjust quantities and send again.

Receiving

How do I receive a purchase order, including partial shipments?

Open the PO and click "Receive." Enter the quantity actually received per line item — if it's less than ordered, the PO stays open as "Partially Received" and the remaining quantity stays outstanding until you receive it (or close the PO manually if you're not expecting the rest).

Does receiving update my Shopify inventory automatically?

Yes — quantities you mark as received are added to Shopify inventory for the correct location immediately, no separate sync step needed.

I received into the wrong location — can I fix it?

Yes — open the receiving record and edit the location before saving, or if already saved, create an inventory adjustment/transfer in Shopify directly; Stockloop's receiving record itself can be corrected within 24 hours from the PO's history tab.

What if a supplier sends more than I ordered?

Enter the actual quantity received — Stockloop allows receiving more than ordered and will flag the overage on the PO so you have a record of the discrepancy for reconciling with the supplier invoice.

Suppliers

How do I add a supplier and does it import from Stocky?

Add manually (Suppliers → New) with contact email, lead time, and payment terms, or import from a Stocky CSV export (see Migrating from Stocky). Assign each product to a primary supplier so reorder suggestions and POs know where to route.

Can one product have multiple suppliers?

Yes — assign a primary supplier (used for suggestions and default POs) and optional backup suppliers you can manually select when creating a one-off PO.

Billing & Plans

What's included in each plan, and can I switch anytime?

Free ($0: up to 100 active SKUs, 5 POs/month, 1 location), Starter ($29: up to 1,000 SKUs), Growth ($59: up to 5,000 SKUs, multi-location), Scale ($99: unlimited SKUs, priority support). Paid plans include unlimited purchase orders and reorder suggestions, with no GMV or per-order fees, and a 14-day free trial. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from Settings → Plan; upgrades take effect immediately (Shopify prorates the charge), cancellations stop billing at the end of the current cycle.

What's your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancel anytime, no lock-in — canceling stops all future charges; you keep access until the end of the current billing cycle. We don't issue prorated refunds for partial cycles (standard Shopify Billing behavior), which is why every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Your data (suppliers, PO history) stays exportable as CSV for 30 days after cancellation before deletion.

Data & Privacy

How is my order and inventory data used?

Stockloop only uses your order history to calculate sales velocity and reorder suggestions for your own store — it is never shared, sold, or pooled across merchants. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export or delete your data anytime from Settings → Data.

Does uninstalling delete my data?

Uninstalling stops the app from running but retains your data for 30 days (in case you reinstall), then permanently deletes it. Request immediate deletion anytime via Settings → Data → Delete my data, or by emailing support.

Troubleshooting

Why is my inventory sync delayed?

Stockloop syncs inventory levels from Shopify roughly every few minutes under normal load; bulk edits (large CSV imports, big collection changes) can take up to 30 minutes to fully propagate. If it's been longer, check Settings → Sync Status for an error, or contact support with the affected SKU.

Why is a product missing from Stockloop?

Usually one of: the product is archived or draft in Shopify (Stockloop only tracks active, published products by default — toggle this in Settings), it was added to Shopify in the last few minutes (wait for the next sync), or it has no inventory tracking enabled in Shopify. Check Settings → Sync Status for a per-product sync log.

Why do suggestions look wrong after a bulk CSV import or migration?

Suggestions rely on accurate current stock and supplier lead-time data — right after a large import, double-check that on-hand quantities and lead times imported correctly (Suppliers tab and Products tab), since one bad lead-time value will skew that product's suggestion until corrected.
