How do I install Stockloop and get my first reorder suggestions?

Click "Add app" on the Shopify App Store listing, approve the permissions screen, and you're done — there's no setup wizard to complete. Stockloop automatically imports your last 12 months of order history the moment you install, and it starts calculating sales velocity immediately. Most stores see their first reorder suggestions within a few minutes, though very large catalogs (10,000+ SKUs) can take up to an hour for the initial import to finish. You'll get an email the moment your first suggestions are ready. You don't need to connect suppliers or import anything manually before this happens — that comes next, and only for SKUs where you want Stockloop to generate a purchase order automatically.

Do I need to do anything before Stockloop can work?

No. Stockloop reads your existing order and product history the moment you install, so reorder suggestions appear without configuration. Two optional steps improve accuracy fast: add supplier lead times (Suppliers tab) so suggestions account for how long stock takes to arrive, and set a safety-stock buffer per product or collection if you want more or less cushion than the default. Neither is required to start.

What Shopify plan or POS setup do I need?

Stockloop works on any Shopify plan and does not require Shopify POS or POS Pro. If you sell in multiple physical or online locations, Stockloop supports multi-location inventory and reorder suggestions natively — no separate setup per location.

Which permissions does Stockloop request, and why?

Stockloop requests read access to products, inventory levels, and order history (to calculate velocity and suggestions), and write access to draft purchase orders it creates on your behalf. It never modifies your product listings, prices, or storefront content.

Can I use Stockloop alongside my current inventory or accounting app?

Yes. Stockloop only manages purchase orders, suppliers, and reorder suggestions — it doesn't touch pricing, fulfillment, or accounting. In v1, it does not sync to accounting software; export PO and receiving data as CSV if your bookkeeper needs it.
