How do I receive a purchase order, including partial shipments?

Open the PO and click "Receive." Enter the quantity actually received per line item — if it's less than ordered, the PO stays open as "Partially Received" and the remaining quantity stays outstanding until you receive it (or close the PO manually if you're not expecting the rest).

Does receiving update my Shopify inventory automatically?

Yes — quantities you mark as received are added to Shopify inventory for the correct location immediately, no separate sync step needed.

I received into the wrong location — can I fix it?

Yes — open the receiving record and edit the location before saving, or if already saved, create an inventory adjustment/transfer in Shopify directly; Stockloop's receiving record itself can be corrected within 24 hours from the PO's history tab.

What if a supplier sends more than I ordered?

Enter the actual quantity received — Stockloop allows receiving more than ordered and will flag the overage on the PO so you have a record of the discrepancy for reconciling with the supplier invoice.
