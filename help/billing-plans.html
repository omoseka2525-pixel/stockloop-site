What's included in each plan, and can I switch anytime?

Free ($0: up to 100 active SKUs, 5 POs/month, 1 location), Starter ($29: up to 1,000 SKUs), Growth ($59: up to 5,000 SKUs, multi-location), Scale ($99: unlimited SKUs, priority support). Paid plans include unlimited purchase orders and reorder suggestions, with no GMV or per-order fees, and a 14-day free trial. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from Settings → Plan; upgrades take effect immediately (Shopify prorates the charge), cancellations stop billing at the end of the current cycle.

What's your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancel anytime, no lock-in — canceling stops all future charges; you keep access until the end of the current billing cycle. We don't issue prorated refunds for partial cycles (standard Shopify Billing behavior), which is why every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Your data (suppliers, PO history) stays exportable as CSV for 30 days after cancellation before deletion.
