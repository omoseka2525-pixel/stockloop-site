How do I add a supplier and does it import from Stocky?

Add manually (Suppliers → New) with contact email, lead time, and payment terms, or import from a Stocky CSV export (see Migrating from Stocky). Assign each product to a primary supplier so reorder suggestions and POs know where to route.

Can one product have multiple suppliers?

Yes — assign a primary supplier (used for suggestions and default POs) and optional backup suppliers you can manually select when creating a one-off PO.
