Stockloop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 · Applies to the Stockloop app for Shopify merchants ("Stockloop", "we", "us").

This policy explains what data Stockloop collects when a merchant installs the app, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how merchants and their customers can exercise their data rights. Stockloop is built for Shopify merchants (referred to here as "merchants" or "you") and, where relevant, their customers ("customers").

1. Data we collect

CategoryWhat it includesWhy we collect it
Shop informationShop domain, plan, contact email, currency, and location listTo identify your store, run the app inside Shopify admin, and support multi-location inventory
Product & inventory dataProduct and variant titles, SKUs, and per-location inventory levelsTo match reorder suggestions and purchase orders to the correct product and stock level
Order dataOrder line items and quantities (not full customer order records) going back up to 12 monthsUsed solely to calculate sales velocity and reorder suggestions
Supplier contactsSupplier name, email, phone, lead time, and terms — entered directly by the merchantTo generate, send, and track purchase orders with your suppliers
Stocky migration filesCSV exports you choose to upload from Stocky (suppliers, products, purchase order history)To reconcile your prior Stocky data against your current Shopify inventory during migration

Order history is used solely to calculate reorder suggestions and is not used for any other purpose, including advertising.

2. What we don't do with your data

  • We do not sell merchant or customer data to third parties.
  • We do not pool or share one merchant's sales, inventory, or supplier data with another merchant.
  • We do not use your data to train third-party AI models.
  • Stockloop never modifies your product listings, prices, or storefront content — it only reads product/order/inventory data and writes purchase orders it creates on your behalf.

3. Where data is stored

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Application data is hosted with our infrastructure provider in United States (Ashburn, Virginia). If your business is subject to a specific data-residency requirement, contact us at support@stockloop.io before installing.

4. Data retention

While the app is installed, we retain data for as long as needed to provide the service. If you uninstall Stockloop, your data is retained for 30 days (in case you reinstall), then permanently deleted, consistent with the shop/redact webhook described below. You can request immediate deletion at any time from Settings → Data → Delete my data, or by emailing support.

5. Mandatory Shopify GDPR webhooks

As a public Shopify app, Stockloop subscribes to and honors Shopify's three mandatory compliance webhooks:

  • customers/data_request — when a merchant or their customer requests a copy of stored data, we provide what we hold (e.g., any order-derived data tied to that customer) within the timeframe Shopify's guidance requires.
  • customers/redact — when a merchant or customer requests deletion, we erase or redact the customer's data, consistent with Shopify's guidance on any applicable order-related retention window.
  • shop/redact — fires automatically after uninstall; we erase all data tied to that shop on the schedule described in Section 4.

All three endpoints validate Shopify's X-Shopify-Hmac-Sha256 signature header before processing a request.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA baseline)

Depending on where you or your customers are located, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Merchants can exercise these rights for their own account directly in Settings → Data, or by contacting us. If a merchant's customer wants to exercise these rights, they should contact the merchant directly (as the data controller for their own store) — the merchant can then relay the request to us via the mandatory webhooks described above, or by emailing support.

8. Children's data

Stockloop is a business tool for merchants and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about children.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above when this policy changes and, for material changes, notify merchants by email or in-app notice.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or a data request: support@stockloop.io. We aim to respond within 1 business day.
