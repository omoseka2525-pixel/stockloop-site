Trial, billing & cancellation policy

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Starter, Growth, and Scale each include a 14-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel any time during the trial with nothing owed. The Free plan has no trial because it has no charge.

What's your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancel anytime, no lock-in — canceling stops all future charges; you keep access until the end of the current billing cycle. We don't issue prorated refunds for partial cycles (standard Shopify Billing behavior), which is why every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

What happens to my data after I cancel?

Your data (suppliers, PO history) stays exportable as CSV for 30 days after cancellation, then it is deleted. Request immediate deletion anytime via Settings → Data → Delete my data, or by emailing support.

Billing FAQ

What's included in each plan, and can I switch anytime?

Free ($0: up to 100 active SKUs, 5 POs/month, 1 location), Starter ($29: up to 1,000 SKUs), Growth ($59: up to 5,000 SKUs, multi-location), Scale ($99: unlimited SKUs, priority support). Paid plans include unlimited purchase orders and reorder suggestions, with no GMV or per-order fees, and a 14-day free trial. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from Settings → Plan; upgrades take effect immediately (Shopify prorates the charge), cancellations stop billing at the end of the current cycle.

Does Stockloop cost more than Stocky, which was free?

Stockloop is a paid app because it's independently built and maintained (Stocky was subsidized as part of Shopify POS Pro). Pricing is flat — Free, $29, $59, or $99/month depending on catalog size and locations — with no per-order or per-PO fees, so your cost doesn't scale with sales volume the way some alternatives' usage-based pricing does.

How is billing actually charged?

All charges run through Shopify's own Billing system, shown on your regular Shopify invoice — Stockloop never charges your card directly. You can upgrade or downgrade from inside the app at any time without contacting support.
