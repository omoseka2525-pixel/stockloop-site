Stocky migration FAQ

The questions we hear most from merchants switching before the August 31, 2026 shutdown.

Shopify is sunsetting Stocky on August 31, 2026. Does Stockloop replace everything Stocky did?

Stockloop covers the core of what most merchants used Stocky for: purchase orders, receiving (including partial receiving), reorder suggestions based on sales velocity and lead time, a supplier master, and low-stock alerts. If you relied on a Stocky feature outside that scope, contact support before you migrate so we can confirm fit.

How do I bring my Stocky data into Stockloop?

Export your suppliers, products, and purchase order history from Stocky as CSV files, then upload them in Stockloop's Import screen. Stockloop reconciles each row against your current Shopify inventory and flags anything it can't match automatically so you can review before committing.

Will I lose any data during the switch?

The import is reconciliation-first: nothing is written to your store until you review the match summary and confirm. Rows that don't match cleanly are held for manual review rather than silently dropped or silently applied.

Do I need to do this before August 31, 2026?

Yes — Shopify has confirmed Stocky will stop functioning on that date. We recommend migrating at least two weeks earlier so you have time to verify supplier records and in-flight purchase orders before Stocky access ends.

What happens to purchase orders that are still open in Stocky when I switch?

Include open POs in your CSV export. Stockloop imports them with their current received/outstanding quantities so you can continue receiving against them without re-creating them from scratch.

Does Stockloop cost more than Stocky did?

Stocky was bundled with certain Shopify plans at no separate charge. Stockloop is a paid app with a free tier (up to 100 SKUs) and flat monthly plans from $29–$99, with no percentage-of-sales or per-order fees — so cost scales with your SKU count and order volume, not your revenue.

How long does setup actually take?

There is no manual setup step. After you install and approve the requested permissions, Stockloop imports your order history automatically and produces your first reorder suggestions within minutes. Supplier and lead-time details can be added at your own pace afterward.

Who do I contact if something looks wrong after migrating?

Email support is included on every plan, including Free, with a stated response-time target. If a reconciliation result looks wrong, include your Stocky export file and the affected SKU or PO number so we can trace it quickly.
