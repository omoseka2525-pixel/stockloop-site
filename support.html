What to include in your ticket

The faster we can trace your issue, the faster we can fix it. Where relevant, please include:

  • ✓ Your Shopify store domain (e.g. your-store.myshopify.com)
  • ✓ The affected SKU, product name, or purchase order number
  • ✓ If it's a reorder suggestion question: which product, and what looks wrong about it
  • ✓ If it's a Stocky migration question: the CSV export file and an example row that didn't match
  • ✓ If it's a billing question: your current plan and what you expected vs. what you saw
